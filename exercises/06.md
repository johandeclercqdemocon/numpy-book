# Exercises — Chapter 6

Chapter: [Ufuncs and vectorization](../chapters/06-ufuncs-and-vectorization.md) · module: [`examples/ch06_ufuncs.py`](../examples/ch06_ufuncs.py) · solutions: [06](solutions/06.md)

**1. [measure]** For 100,000 values, measure the ratio of the time of `ma_window` to the time of `ma_cumsum` at window 10 and at window 1000. Expected: the ratio at window 1000 is above 10, and it is larger than the ratio at window 10. Say why the ratio depends on the window.

**2. [break]** `np.vectorize(lambda v: 0 if v < 1 else v)(np.array([0.5, 2.7]))` must give `[0. 2.7]`. Run it and report the result. Then repair it with one argument. Expected: the result `[0 2]` first, and `[0. 2.7]` after the repair.

**3. [judge]** A colleague wants to rewrite the exponential moving average of chapter 6 in Numba. What does a good answer weigh? (Consider the 3.5 million steps a second that one run of chapter 6 printed, the size of the real data, the cost of a new dependency, and what a profile must show first.)
