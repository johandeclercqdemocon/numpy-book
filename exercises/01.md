# Exercises — Chapter 1

Chapter: [The array](../chapters/01-the-array.md) · module: [`examples/ch01_array.py`](../examples/ch01_array.py) · solutions: [01](solutions/01.md)

**1. [measure]** Make `np.zeros((100, 200, 3), dtype=np.float32)`. Report its `strides` and its `nbytes`. Expected: strides `(2400, 12, 4)` and `nbytes` 240000, exact.

**2. [break]** Chapter 1 says that a transpose is not C-contiguous. Find a 2-D array for which the transpose is still C-contiguous. Explain why the flag stays `True`. Expected: a shape with an axis of length 1, for example `(1, 5)`.

**3. [judge]** A colleague stores 20 sensor readings per request in a NumPy array, and the service handles thousands of requests each second. Another colleague says that a plain list is better. What does a good answer weigh? (Consider the fixed cost per call from chapter 1, what the readings are used for next, and what a measurement on the real service must show.)
