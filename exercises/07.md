# Exercises — Chapter 7

Chapter: [Linear algebra and random numbers](../chapters/07-linear-algebra-and-random.md) · module: [`examples/ch07_linalg_random.py`](../examples/ch07_linalg_random.py) · solutions: [07](solutions/07.md)

**1. [measure]** Compute `np.linalg.cond` of the 10-by-10 Hilbert matrix. Expected: 1.6e13, within a factor of 2.

**2. [break]** `np.linalg.solve` gave an error of 1.2e-7 for the 8-by-8 Hilbert system in `float64`. Change only the dtype of the inputs so that the error is larger than 1. Expected: `float32` gives an error near 7. Explain the result with the condition number and the `float32` epsilon.

**3. [judge]** A team of five runs experiments. Each script has `np.random.seed(0)` at the top. The scripts import shared helper functions that also draw random numbers. What does a good answer weigh before the team changes its practice? (Consider hidden coupling, reproducibility across versions, and the cost of passing a generator through every function.)
