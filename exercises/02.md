# Exercises — Chapter 2

Chapter: [Dtypes, and the answers that change silently](../chapters/02-dtypes.md) · module: [`examples/ch02_dtypes.py`](../examples/ch02_dtypes.py) · solutions: [02](solutions/02.md)

**1. [measure]** Find the smallest positive integer `n` for which `np.float32(n) + np.float32(1) == np.float32(n)`. Expected: 16777216, exact.

**2. [break]** The cumulative sum of ten million `float32` values of 0.1 is wrong by 8.8 percent in chapter 2. Change one argument of `np.cumsum`, and nothing else, so that the last value is within 1e-6 (relative) of 1,000,000.015. Expected: `dtype=np.float64`, and a relative error below 1e-6.

**3. [judge]** A library function adds two `uint8` images. What must it do on overflow: wrap, clip at 255, or raise an error? What does a good answer weigh? (Consider who calls the function, what a wrapped pixel looks like, and what each choice costs in speed and in surprise.)
